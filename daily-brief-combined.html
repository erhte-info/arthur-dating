Creatine isn't a gym supplement — it's fuel for your brain
肌酸不是健身品,是大腦的燃料
Science · 科學
Creatine recharges ATP — your cells' energy currency — within seconds via phosphocreatine, a "fast battery." Neurons lean on it under stress, poor sleep and heavy focus. Supplementing lifts brain phosphocreatine 10–15%.
肌酸透過磷酸肌酸(細胞的「快充電池」)在幾秒內把能量貨幣 ATP 充回來。大腦在高壓、睡不足、專注時特別依賴它;補充能讓腦內磷酸肌酸提升 10–15%。
For you · 對你
Clearer thinking when you're under-slept; better memory, attention and processing speed under a heavy workload.
睡不夠時思緒更清楚;高工作量下記憶、注意力、反應速度更好——不容易「腦當機」。
For women · 對女性
Women store 70–80% less creatine than men, and the link to mood and brain energy sharpens around menstruation, pregnancy and menopause — so the upside may be even bigger. 3–5 g/day, safe and cheap.
女性肌酸儲存量比男性低 70–80%,且在月經、懷孕、更年期等荷爾蒙波動期,與情緒和腦能量的關聯更明顯,邊際效益可能更大。每日 3–5g,安全又便宜。

002
代謝智能Gut-BrainMood
Two servings of kimchi a day quiets your brain's alarm
一天兩份泡菜,把大腦的警報器關小
Science · 科學
Your gut and brain talk over the "gut-brain axis." Live-culture foods (kimchi, kefir, miso, kombucha) raise microbial diversity, lower inflammation, and boost GABA — the neurotransmitter that hits the brakes on your brain — calming the amygdala, your fear-and-threat center.
腸道和大腦透過「腸腦軸」對話。發酵食物(泡菜、克菲爾、味噌、康普茶)提升腸道菌多樣性、降發炎,並促進 GABA(讓大腦踩剎車的神經傳導物質),安撫杏仁核——你的恐懼與威脅中樞。
For you · 對你
A food lever for mood and stress, not just digestion. Aim for 1–2 servings daily (4–6 while recovering from antibiotics).
這是用食物調情緒與壓力的槓桿,不只助消化。每天 1–2 份(抗生素後恢復期 4–6 份)。

003
長壽基礎VO2 MaxPerformance
One number predicts your death better than cholesterol
有一個數字比膽固醇更能預測你哪天死
Science · 科學
VO2 max is the most oxygen your body can use at peak effort — a single score for your whole engine: heart, lungs, blood, vessels and mitochondria. Large studies (JAMA 2018) found the least-fit face several-fold higher mortality — a bigger risk than smoking or diabetes.
VO2 max 是你極限時每分鐘能利用的最大氧氣量——是你整台引擎(心、肺、血液、血管、粒線體)的總分。大型研究(JAMA 2018)發現體能最差的一群死亡風險高出數倍,甚至大於抽菸與糖尿病。
For you · 對你
A high VO2 max = more physiological reserve to survive illness, injury and aging. Build it with Attia's 4×4: 4–6 rounds of 3–4 min at 90–95% max heart rate, on a Zone 2 base.
VO2 max 高 = 更多生理儲備,生病、受傷、老化時更撐得住。用 Attia 的 4×4:4–6 組、每組 3–4 分鐘衝到 90–95% 最大心率,搭 Zone 2 打底。

Your memory peaks right before ovulation
妳的記憶力在排卵前達到高峰
Science · 科學
A 2026 study tracking cognition across the menstrual cycle found memory and attention peak in the late follicular phase — when estrogen, which strengthens neural connections, is at its highest.
一項 2026 研究追蹤月經週期的認知變化,發現記憶與注意力在濾泡期後段(雌激素高峰)表現最好——雌激素被認為能強化神經連結。
For women · 對女性
Where you can, aim your hardest cognitive work — big pitches, learning, decisions — at this window, and schedule recovery and low-stakes tasks premenstrually. This is cycle syncing's most evidence-backed piece.
盡量把最耗腦的事(重要簡報、學習、決策)排進這個高峰段,低谷期(經前)安排恢復性、低壓的任務。這是 cycle syncing 最有實證的一塊。

005
恢復與生物優化Urolithin AMitochondria
The compound that swaps out your cells' dead batteries
幫細胞換掉壞電池的那個成分
Science · 科學
Urolithin A isn't something you eat directly — your gut bacteria make it from polyphenols in pomegranate, walnuts and berries. But only ~40% of people carry the right bacteria to produce enough. It switches on "mitophagy" — recycling worn-out mitochondria (your cells' power plants) for fresh ones.
Urolithin A 不是你直接吃到的——腸道細菌把石榴、核桃、莓果裡的多酚分解後才產生。但只有約 40% 的人有對的菌能製造足量。它啟動「粒線體自噬(mitophagy)」——回收老舊粒線體(細胞的發電廠)、換上新的。
For you · 對你
A supplement route to muscle strength, endurance and cellular aging — relevant if you sit a lot and want to defend your energy. A 2026 review links it to better strength and fitness.
一條主打肌力、耐力與細胞抗老的補充路徑——久坐、想守住體能的人特別相關。2026 年一篇回顧連結它與肌力和體能的改善。
